Rules reference

Keeper salaries & the escalator

No keeper limit, no salary cap. Keeper salaries come out of the $260 auction budget. A player’s cost is his previous salary plus the escalator for his next year of service:

Yr 1  —Yr 2  +$1Yr 3  +$3 Yr 4  +$5Yr 5  +$7Yr 6+  +$5

Service time resets on any trade. A new owner’s first year keeping a player is Year 1, whatever ladder the previous owner had climbed.

Official salaries are the ones in this ledger. ESPN’s displayed salaries are not binding.

Winter Meetings exception

Current Year Salaries hold until 12:00 AM PT at the close of Winter Meetings day, when Next Year Salaries take effect.

A trade made during the 24-hour Winter Meetings window waives the escalator for the upcoming season — you get the player at his existing salary, and he takes the +$1 only if you keep him the year after. Such a player carries Year 0 until the season starts.

Trades before or after that window follow the normal reset: new owner at Year 1, +$1 the following season. You cannot reacquire a player in the same league year to benefit from the reset.

Free agency & FAAB

FAAB budget is $100 plus whatever is left of your draft budget. Minimum bid $3. Bids process Sunday and Monday at 8:00 AM; ties break on waiver priority, set weekly worst to first.

In-season FAAB can be included in trades. Acquired FAAB expires at the end of the regular waiver period.

Trades & cash

Trade deadline is two weeks before the playoffs. Trades process immediately; the commissioner reviews trade-backs and collusion.

Cash may be included as an asset or liability with a defined duration — for example $15 over five years, recorded as five separate $3 records. Each year’s balance adjusts that team’s draft budget right after the keeper deadline. Nothing is currently outstanding.

Commissioner’s Exempt List

A player suspended for non-baseball reasons — crimes or investigations, not PEDs — may be cut while his owner keeps his rights. Escalators keep accruing.

On reinstatement, every other owner may submit a blind bid and the original owner may match the highest. If the original owner takes him back, he pays the escalated salary. If someone else wins, the winning bid becomes the new base. The winner must keep him through the draft.

Prizes

$100 buy-in. Playoff champion $400, runner-up $200, third $100, best regular-season record $250, consolation champion $100, top scorer over the final seven weeks $20.

Consolation is a three-week Royal Rumble — you play every team every week, most total wins takes the $100. Weekly consolation winners get $3 of draft/FAAB money. Last place (the BooB) owes $40 toward updating the trophy.
